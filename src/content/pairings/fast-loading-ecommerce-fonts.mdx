## Why these pairings work

Typography is about more than just picking fonts that look good. It's about psychology and function. For **fast loading ecommerce fonts**, the key is balance. You want to create enough contrast to guide the eye, but enough harmony to make the experience feel unified.

Professional designers look at the "x-height" and the "optical weight" of fonts when pairing them. If you want a design that feels cohesive, try to match the underlying structure of the letters, even if the styles are different.
