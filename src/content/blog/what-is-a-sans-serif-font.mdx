# What is a Sans-Serif Font?

A sans-serif font is a typeface that lacks the small decorative strokes (serifs) at the ends of its letterforms. "Sans" is French for "without." These fonts are celebrated for their clean, modern, minimalist, and approachable aesthetic, making them the dominant choice for digital design.

## How Does It Work?

Sans-serif fonts provide a clean, uncluttered look that renders exceptionally well on digital screens, even at low resolutions. Their simple, geometric letterforms make them highly legible at small sizes, which is why they are the standard for user interfaces and mobile applications.

## Real-World Example in Industry

Digital interfaces, such as the menus on your smartphone, the dashboard of a SaaS application, or the navigation bar of a website, heavily rely on sans-serif fonts (like Inter, Roboto, or Apple's San Francisco) to ensure maximum clarity and usability.
