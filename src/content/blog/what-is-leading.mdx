# What is Leading?

Leading (pronounced "ledding"), also known as line height in digital design, is the vertical space measured from the baseline of one line of text to the baseline of the line immediately above or below it. It is a critical factor in determining the overall readability and texture of a paragraph.

## How Does It Work?

Leading adjusts the breathing room between lines. If the leading is too tight, the text feels cramped and the eye struggles to track from the end of one line to the beginning of the next. If it is too loose, the lines appear disconnected. Optimal leading improves the reader's flow.

## Real-World Example in Industry

For long paragraphs of body text on a blog or article, designers typically set the leading (line height) to around 140% to 160% of the font size. This provides enough vertical space for the reader's eye to comfortably navigate the block of text without losing their place.
