# FONTFUSION CONTENT ENGINE: THE 6 REUSABLE TEMPLATES

This document defines the 6 core templates used to build massive semantic surface area for FontFusion.

---

## Template A – Cluster Article (700-1000 words)
**Goal:** Daily educational depth and topical authority.

- **H1:** `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`: Complete Guide for `[TARGET_AUDIENCE]` in 2026
- **AI Snapshot:** (30-word intro definition for Google AI Overview)
- **H2:** Why `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` Matters for `[AUDIENCE/USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** How to `[ACTION]` with `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` (step-by-step)
- **H2:** Top `[NUMBER]` `[STRATEGIES/TOOLS]` for `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`
- **H3:** Detailed breakdown of each (with real font examples)
- **H2:** Common Mistakes to Avoid (with table)
- **AEO Box:** 5 Key Takeaways (bullets)
- **AEO Box:** FAQ Section with 5 questions + FAQPage Schema
- **Internal Links:** 1 Pillar + 2 Clusters + Generator Tool

---

## Template B – Programmatic Comparison Page (500-700 words)
**Goal:** Hijack competitor search intent and scale infinitely.

- **H1:** `[ENTITY_A]` vs `[ENTITY_B]`: Which Font Pairing Tool is Better for `[USE_CASE]`?
- **Quick Verdict:** 1-sentence AI summary
- **Comparison Table:** (Features, `[ENTITY_A]`, `[ENTITY_B]`, Winner)
- **Deep Dive [ENTITY_A]:** (Pros/Cons)
- **Deep Dive [ENTITY_B]:** (Pros/Cons)
- **When to choose each**
- **AEO Box:** "Best Pick for [Scenario]"
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Generator

---

## Template C – Micro-Answer Page (300-500 words)
**Goal:** Featured snippets and voice search optimization.

- **H1:** What is `[TYPOGRAPHY_TERM]`? (direct question format)
- **Definition:** 40-60 word definition
- **H2:** How Does `[TERM]` Work?
- **H2:** Real-World Example of `[TERM]` in `[INDUSTRY]`
- **H2:** Why is `[TERM]` Important?
- **H2:** `[TERM]` vs `[RELATED_TERM]` (comparison table)
- **AEO Box:** Core Principles (bullets)
- **Internal Links:** 1 Cluster + Generator

---

## Template D – Pillar Page (3000-7000 words)
**Goal:** The ultimate weekly hub and topical cornerstone.

- **H1:** The Ultimate 2026 Guide to `[BROAD_TOPIC]`
- **Executive Summary:** 200 words for AI Overviews
- **Table of Contents:** with jump links
- **Chapters:** Introduction/Evolution, Technical Foundation, Top 10 Strategies, Case Study, Future Trends
- **AEO Box:** Statistical Data Table (e.g., most popular font pairings, usage statistics)
- **AEO Box:** 10+ FAQ with Schema
- **Internal Links:** 15+ Cluster Articles + Generator

---

## Template E – Mood/Use-Case Collection Page (600-800 words)
**Goal:** Commercial and inspirational intent (Scaling).

- **H1:** `[NUMBER]` `[MOOD/VIBE]` Font Pairings for `[INDUSTRY/PROJECT]` in 2026
- **Introduction:** Why `[MOOD]` typography works for `[INDUSTRY/PROJECT]`
- **5 Curated Font Pairings:**
  - Font names + foundry links
  - Why they work together (contrast, hierarchy, mood)
  - Sample CSS/Google Fonts import
  - Real-world usage examples
- **How to apply these pairings**
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table (Pairing Name → Fonts → Mood → Best For)
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Generator + Category

---

## Template F – Industry/Project-Specific Guide (600-800 words)
**Goal:** Capturing high-intent commercial queries.

- **H1:** The Best Font Pairings for `[INDUSTRY/PROJECT_TYPE]` in 2026
- **Introduction:** Why typography matters in `[INDUSTRY/PROJECT_TYPE]`
- **Top 5 Font Pairings for [INDUSTRY/PROJECT_TYPE]**
- **How to implement these pairings**
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table (Use Case → Recommended Pairing → Why It Works)
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Generator + Mood Collection
