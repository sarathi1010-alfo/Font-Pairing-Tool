# THE ETERNAL WEEKLY STRATEGY

This document outlines the repeatable rhythm and topic queue for the FontFusion content engine.

## THE REPEATABLE WEEKLY CALENDAR

| Day | Theme | Deliverable Type |
|-----|-------|------------------|
| **Monday** | Typography Fundamentals | 3-5 Daily Clusters (Template A) |
| **Tuesday** | Competitor Comparisons | 1-2 Comparisons (Template B) |
| **Wednesday** | Mood-Based Collections | 5-10 Programmatic (Template E) |
| **Thursday** | Industry-Specific Guides | 1-2 Industry Guides (Template F) |
| **Friday** | Technical Implementation | 5 Micro-Answers (Template C) |
| **Saturday** | Planning & Interlinking | Audit internal links + 1 Weekly Pillar (Template D) |
| **Sunday** | Pruning & Refreshing | Refresh metadata + Designer Spotlight |

---

## THE ETERNAL WEEKLY RHYTHM (CADENCE)

- **Weekly Pillar:** 1 per week
- **Weekly Mood/Use-Case Collections:** 2–3 per week
- **Weekly Industry Guides:** 1–2 per week
- **Weekly Comparisons:** 1–2 per week
- **Daily Clusters:** 3–5 per day
- **Daily Programmatic:** 5–10 per day (mood/use-case collections)
- **Daily Micro-Answers:** 5 per day
- **Weekly Target:** 50–100 new pages indexed
