# FontFusion Content Engine: Eternal Strategy

This document outlines the repeatable weekly rhythm and strategy for building massive semantic surface area for FontFusion.

## 1. The Eternal Weekly Rhythm

Our content production follows a strictly defined cadence to ensure consistent growth across authority clusters and programmatic intent.

- **Weekly Pillar:** 1 high-authority long-form guide per week.
- **Weekly Mood/Use-Case Collections:** 2–3 curated collections per week.
- **Weekly Industry Guides:** 1–2 specific industry deep-dives per week.
- **Weekly Comparisons:** 1–2 programmatic comparison pages per week.
- **Daily Clusters:** 3–5 supporting educational articles per day.
- **Daily Programmatic:** 5–10 automated mood/use-case pages per day.
- **Daily Micro-Answers:** 5 snippet-targeted Q&A pages per day.

**Weekly Target:** 50–100 new pages indexed.
